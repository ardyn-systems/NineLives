Welcome to NineLives

Recover passwords from hashes and Wi-Fi captures with hashcat — no command line required. Here's the whole flow:

  1. 1
    Pick what to crack. Choose a hash file on the Crack tab, or drop a .pcap/.cap on the Captures tab and click Use in Crack.
  2. 2
    Confirm the hash type. Loading a capture fills this in for you. Otherwise start in ★ Common, or search by name or number (e.g. WPA, NTLM, 1000).
  3. 3
    Choose an attack. Straight (dictionary) is the usual starting point. Pick a wordlist — grab bigger ones from the ⚙ Settings dialog.
  4. 4
    Run it. Press Run crack and watch the plain-language status bar. Need the raw hashcat output? Click Show console.
  5. 5
    Get results. Cracked passwords show in the status bar and under Show recovered.

New here? The demo loads a bundled Wi-Fi capture (Coherer). Crack it to recover the password “Induction.”
